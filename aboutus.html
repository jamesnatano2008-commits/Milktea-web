📖 Our Story

Welcome to our Milktea Shop! Born and brewed in Bacoor, Cavite. We started with a simple passion - to serve the freshest, most delicious milktea that warms your heart and brightens your day. From our humble beginnings, we've grown with one goal: to bring joy in every sip!

🎯 Our Mission

To serve high-quality, affordable, and refreshing milktea made with premium ingredients - bringing happiness and smiles to our community, one cup at a time!

✨ Our Vision

To be everyone's favorite local milktea shop — known for great taste, friendly service, and quality you can trust.

💛 What We Stand For

🍵 Fresh & Premium Tea
🍫 Quality Ingredients
🤝 Friendly Service
❤️ Made with Love

🌟 Why Choose Us?

We don't just serve milktea - we craft happiness in a cup. Every drink is handcrafted, carefully prepared, and made fresh just for you. Whether it's classic taro, rich chocolate, or creamy matcha - there's always something special waiting for you here!
